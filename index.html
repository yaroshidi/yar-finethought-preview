Services

One engagement, six parts

  1. 01. Audit and strategy: Know which fix to make first. Includes Page review, baseline, roadmap.
  2. 02. Design and development: Current pages with a clear route to an enquiry. Includes Page design, speed, mobile, components.
  3. 03. SEO and AI search: Easier to find in Google and in AI answers. Includes Buyer questions, technical fixes, internal links.
  4. 04. Lead capture and handoff: Every enquiry reaches a named owner in sales. Includes Form to CRM, lead owner, failure alerts.
  5. 05. Measurement and a monthly report: See what the site brings in each month. Includes Accepted enquiries, monthly report, baseline.
  6. 06. Monthly strategy and delivery: A site that stays current for buyers, search engines and AI tools. Includes Weekly review, work plan, one priority at a time.
