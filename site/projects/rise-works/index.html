Rise Works

Payroll platform that has processed over $1 billion in pay, backed by Draper Associates.

The Webflow marketing site I built for Rise in 2022, set up so a non-technical team could run it after handover.

Role

Webflow development

What I built

  • I built Rise a new marketing site in Webflow in April and May 2022. I set it up so a non-technical team could run it after handover. It replaced Rise's previous site on riseworks.io in late May 2022.
  • Five use cases got their own pages: finance and operations, HR and people, legal and compliance, DAOs and contractors. The site also had product pages, pricing, a blog, an integrations directory and a demo request page.
  • Blog posts and integrations went into the CMS, each with its own page. New pages could start from a landing page template or an inner page template.
  • The site stayed on that Webflow project for over four years, until Rise moved it to Framer in August 2026.

Details

  • Seven integrations at launch, including Arbitrum, Uniswap, MetaMask and Gnosis Safe. Each had its own page, and type pages grouped them into wallets, chains and DeFi protocols.
  • The integrations in the menu and the blog posts on the homepage were CMS lists, so they updated from the CMS without anyone editing those pages.
  • The landing page template had the lead form, customer quotes and stats in place. The inner page template had the header, a rich text block and the footer.
  • On the launch homepage, a line above the client logos cycled through founders, accountants, financial managers, compliance managers and HR managers, and tabs walked through six use cases.
